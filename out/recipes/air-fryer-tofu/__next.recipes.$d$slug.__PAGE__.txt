a:T62f,Tofu in the air fryer is a revelation for anyone who has struggled with soggy, bland tofu on the stovetop. The circulating hot air crisps every surface of the cube evenly, producing a crunch that rivals deep frying — without a pot of oil or the mess that comes with it. The key is pressing the tofu first to remove excess water, then marinating it briefly so it absorbs flavor before the cornstarch coating goes on.

Most tofu recipes fail for one of two reasons: not enough seasoning or not enough texture. This recipe solves both. The soy-garlic-ginger marinade saturates the tofu with umami, and the cornstarch coating creates a thin, crispy shell that shatters when you bite into it. The contrast between the crunchy exterior and the soft, creamy interior is what makes this recipe work.

Pressing the tofu is non-negotiable. Tofu is packed with water, and that water prevents both seasoning absorption and crispiness. Wrapping it in paper towels and pressing it under a heavy object for 15 to 30 minutes removes enough moisture to transform the texture completely. If you cook tofu straight from the package without pressing, no amount of cornstarch will save it.

In the Gulf, tofu has found a growing audience among health-conscious eaters and the vegetarian and vegan communities across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and the wider region. This recipe works as a protein for grain bowls, a stir-fry component, a salad topper, or simply as a snack with dipping sauce.

The circulating heat hits every surface of each floret, crisping the edges to a deep golden brown while keeping the interior tender and slightly nutty. In a conventional oven, this level of caramelisation takes 30 to 40 minutes and usually requires flipping. In the air fryer, you get there in 15 minutes with a single shake of the basket.

The garlic and parmesan here are not just seasoning — they are architecture. The parmesan melts into a thin, crispy crust on the surface of each floret, and the garlic toasts to a sweet, mellow flavor that bears no resemblance to the sharp bite of raw garlic. Together, they transform plain cauliflower into something that genuinely competes with fries and chips for snacking appeal.

The trick to great air fryer cauliflower is not overcrowding the basket. Cauliflower releases moisture as it cooks, and if the florets are packed in too tightly, they steam instead of roasting. The difference between crowded cauliflower (soft, pale, slightly mushy) and properly spaced cauliflower (crispy, golden, caramelised) is dramatic.

In Gulf kitchens, cauliflower has always been popular — used in everything from Indian-influenced gobi dishes to Arabic-style fried cauliflower with tahini. The air fryer version adds a Western twist with parmesan while keeping the simplicity that makes cauliflower a weeknight staple.
