Brush with 2 tbsp olive oil, season, and air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 15 to 18 minutes without turning, until the edges are dark golden and crunchy. Lift them out with a fish slice.</p>

<li><strong>Overcrowding.</strong> Hot air needs a path around each piece. Single layer for halves, two layers at most for cubes.</li>
<li><strong>Halves face up.</strong> The cut face browns best against the preheated basket, so put halves cut side down.</li>
<li><strong>Salting too early.</strong> Salt draws water out. Season just before cooking, not 20 minutes ahead.</li>
