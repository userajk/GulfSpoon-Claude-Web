Air Fryer Asparagus with Lemon Garlic Oil

By Sarah Al-Fayed|Updated: November 28, 2026
Prep 5 mins
Cook 8 mins
Total 13 mins
Servings 4
Difficulty Easy
Cuisine International

Asparagus spears roasted in the air fryer until bright green, tender at the stalk and lightly blistered at the tips, then tossed in a warm lemon and garlic oil. Spear thickness sets the cooking time, so the chart below gives times from pencil-thin to jumbo.

Quick answer

Air fry trimmed, oiled asparagus at 200°C (400°F) for 6 to 8 minutes for medium spears about 1cm thick, shaking halfway. Thin spears take 4 to 6 minutes and thick spears 8 to 10. They are done when bright green, lightly browned at the tips, and the stalk bends slightly when lifted.

Air fryer asparagus is mostly a question of thickness. A pencil-thin spear is cooked through in about 4 minutes. A thick spear the width of your thumb needs twice that, and its woody base benefits from a quick peel. Most recipes give one time for every bunch, which is why so many people end up with limp, olive-green spears or squeaky raw ones. Measure one spear at its thickest point and use the chart below.

The other change from most recipes is the garlic. Raw minced garlic tossed with the spears before cooking sits on the hottest surface for 6 to 8 minutes and scorches. Here the spears go in with only oil and salt. The garlic is stirred into a little warm olive oil with lemon zest and poured over the asparagus as it comes out, so it stays sweet instead of acrid.

Asparagus cooks fast enough to go in after the main dish has finished. Serve it with air fryer salmon, steak bites or a roast chicken.

How long do you cook asparagus in the air fryer at 400°F?

At 200°C (400°F), medium asparagus about 1cm thick takes 6 to 8 minutes. Thin spears take 4 to 6 minutes and thick spears 8 to 10. Those times assume a preheated 5 to 6 litre basket holding about 450g (1 lb) in one layer.

Asparagus is green because of chlorophyll, and long cooking turns it a dull olive colour as the vegetable's own acids react with it. Short, hot cooking keeps the colour bright, which is why 200°C beats a gentler setting here. Only white asparagus, which is fibrous and needs peeling all the way down, does better at 190°C (375°F) for longer.

Does asparagus get crispy in the air fryer? The tips do, a little, and the stalks blister. The stalk itself stays tender rather than crunchy. For a crisp crust all over, use the parmesan or breadcrumb variation below, or wrap bundles of spears in strips of halal turkey bacon using the time in the chart.

Adjust for your machine. In a compact 3 to 4 litre basket, cook half a bunch at a time and start checking 1 minute early. In an oven-style air fryer with racks, use the upper rack and add 1 to 2 minutes.

Choosing and trimming asparagus

Look for firm, straight spears with tightly closed tips and cut ends that look moist, not dry and split. Most asparagus in Gulf supermarkets is imported and has travelled a long way, so it pays to check. If you are not cooking it the same day, stand the bunch upright in a glass with 2cm of water, cover the tops loosely with a bag and keep it in the fridge for up to 3 days.

The woody base is tough no matter how long you cook it. The snap test shows you where it starts, but one snap is enough; cut the rest to match. Thick spears have a fibrous skin on the lower half even above the woody part, so peel it. Thin spears never need peeling.

Pick spears of a similar thickness for one batch. If a bunch is mixed, take the thin ones out 2 minutes before the thick ones.

How to cook frozen asparagus in the air fryer

Cook frozen asparagus straight from the freezer; thawing first makes it limp and watery. Toss the frozen spears with 1 tbsp oil and a pinch of salt, spread them out and cook at 200°C (400°F) for 8 to 10 minutes, shaking twice. Ice melts in the first few minutes, so if water collects in the drawer, tip it out at the first shake.

Frozen asparagus was blanched before freezing, so it will be softer than fresh and will not blister in the same way. It is fine chopped into rice, pasta or an omelette. For more vegetables you can cook from frozen, see our air fryer vegetable time and temperature chart.

Air Fryer Asparagus

Scale:

Ingredients

Asparagus

  • 450 g green asparagus (1 lb, about 1 bunch of medium spears)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp fine salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper (freshly ground)

Lemon Garlic Oil

  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic (finely grated)
  • ½ lemon (finely grated zest, plus 1–2 tsp juice)
  • 1 pinch flaky sea salt (to finish)

Instructions

  1. 1

    Rinse the asparagus and dry it well on a tea towel. Bend one spear near the base until it snaps; it breaks where the woody part ends. Line the rest of the bunch up against it and cut them all to the same length.

    Cutting the bunch to match one snapped spear wastes less than snapping every spear, which often breaks too high.

  2. 2

    Measure the thickness of a spear at its base. If the spears are thicker than 1.5cm (about ⅝ inch), peel the bottom third with a vegetable peeler so the skin does not stay stringy.

  3. 3

    Preheat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F) for 3 minutes. Toss the spears with the olive oil, salt and pepper on a plate or tray, rolling them so each one is coated.

  4. 4

    Lay the spears in the basket in one layer, all pointing the same way. If they do not fit, add a second layer at right angles across the first so air can still pass between them.

  5. 5

    Air fry for 3 minutes, then shake the basket or roll the spears with tongs. Cook for 3 to 5 minutes more for medium spears (see the chart for thin or thick ones).

  6. 6

    Meanwhile, warm the extra virgin olive oil in a small pan over low heat or in the microwave for 15 seconds. Stir in the grated garlic and lemon zest and leave to infuse.

    Warm, not hot. The garlic should soften and smell sweet without sizzling or colouring.

  7. 7

    Test a spear: lift it from the middle with tongs. It is done when both ends droop slightly, the colour is bright green and the tips have a few brown spots. A knife should slide into the thick end with only a little resistance.

  8. 8

    Transfer to a plate, pour over the lemon garlic oil, squeeze over the lemon juice and finish with flaky salt. Serve straight away.

Equipment

Air fryer · Tongs · Vegetable peeler · Small pan · Microplane or fine grater

Tips

  • Dry the spears completely after rinsing. Water on the surface turns to steam and stops the tips browning.
  • Roll the spears in oil on a plate rather than tossing them in a bowl. Long spears snap in a bowl and oil collects at the bottom.
  • Keep the oil to 1 tbsp per 450g. More drips into the drawer and smokes at 200°C.
  • Salt before cooking is fine for asparagus. Unlike mushrooms, it does not release enough water to steam itself.
  • Add the lemon juice just before serving. Acid dulls the green if dressed spears sit around for long.

Troubleshooting

  • Limp, olive-green spears: overcooked. Go by the thickness chart and start checking 1 minute early.
  • Tips burnt, stalks still firm: peel the bases of thick spears and cook at 190°C (375°F) a little longer.
  • Stringy, chewy bases: the woody ends needed trimming, or thick spears needed peeling.
  • Some spears done, others raw: sort the bunch by thickness and pull the thin ones 2 minutes early.
  • Smoke: oil has dripped into the drawer. Keep to 1 tbsp per 450g and roll the spears on a plate rather than tossing them in a bowl.

Substitutions

  • No fresh garlic? Stir ¼ tsp garlic powder into the warm oil instead.
  • Swap lemon for lime zest and juice, or for a pinch of sumac.
  • Butter can replace the extra virgin olive oil in the finishing oil; melt it gently with the garlic.
  • Tenderstem broccoli and green beans can be cooked the same way. Check the chart in our vegetable guide for their times.

Variations

  • Parmesan asparagus: after 5 minutes, sprinkle 3 tbsp finely grated parmesan over the spears and cook 2 minutes more until melted and golden. For halal certification, choose a hard cheese made with microbial rennet.
  • Turkey bacon bundles: wrap bundles of 3 medium spears with a strip of halal turkey bacon, seam side down, and cook at 200°C (400°F) for 9 to 11 minutes until the turkey bacon is crisp.
  • Za'atar and sesame: toss the hot spears with 1 tsp za'atar and 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds instead of the lemon garlic oil.
  • Crumb-coated: toss oiled spears in 4 tbsp panko mixed with lemon zest and salt, and cook 7 to 9 minutes for a crisp coating.
  • Tahini drizzle: whisk 2 tbsp tahini with lemon juice, a little water and a pinch of salt and pour over the cooked spears.

What to Serve With It

  • Alongside air fryer salmon with a spoonful of garlic yoghurt.
  • With steak bites or grilled lamb and roasted baby potatoes.
  • Topped with a soft-boiled egg and shaved parmesan for a light lunch.
  • Chopped and folded into a frittata or a lemony rice salad.

Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Cold leftover asparagus is good chopped into salads and grain bowls.

Reheating

Reheat in the air fryer at 180°C (350°F) for 2 to 3 minutes. Any longer and the spears go limp and lose their colour. Avoid the microwave if you want them firm.

Make Ahead

Trim and peel the asparagus up to 2 days ahead and keep it wrapped in a damp paper towel in a bag in the fridge. Make the lemon garlic oil just before serving, as grated garlic turns harsh if it sits in oil for hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do you cook asparagus in an air fryer?

Cook medium asparagus at 200°C (400°F) for 6 to 8 minutes, shaking halfway. Thin spears under 8mm need 4 to 6 minutes and thick spears over 1.5cm need 8 to 10. Start checking a minute early in a small or powerful air fryer.

What temperature do you cook asparagus in an air fryer?

Use 200°C (400°F) for green and purple asparagus. The high heat cooks the spears quickly so they stay bright green and blister at the tips. White asparagus is better at 190°C (375°F) for 10 to 12 minutes because it is more fibrous.

Do you need to preheat the air fryer for asparagus?

It helps. Asparagus cooks in under 10 minutes, so a cold start adds a noticeable share to the cook time and makes timing less predictable. Three minutes of preheating at 200°C (400°F) is enough in most basket models.

Can you cook frozen asparagus in the air fryer?

Yes, straight from frozen. Toss with oil and salt and cook at 200°C (400°F) for 8 to 10 minutes, shaking twice and pouring off any meltwater. It will be softer than fresh asparagus because it was blanched before freezing.

Why is my air fryer asparagus soggy?

It was overcooked, crowded or wet. Spears piled in a deep heap steam each other, and water left from rinsing does the same. Dry the spears, cook them in a single layer or a criss-cross second layer, and follow the time for their thickness.

Should you peel asparagus before air frying?

Only thick spears. If the base is wider than about 1.5cm, peel the bottom third with a vegetable peeler to remove the fibrous skin. Thin and medium spears only need their woody ends trimmed.
