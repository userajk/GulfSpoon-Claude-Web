Air Fryer Chicken Tenders: Crispy, Juicy & Ready in 20 Minutes

By Sarah Al-Fayed|Updated: July 16, 2026
Prep 10 mins
Cook 14 mins
Total 54 mins
Servings 4
Difficulty Easy
Cuisine International

Crispy, golden chicken tenders with a shattering panko crust and juicy interior, made in the air fryer with barely a tablespoon of oil. A quick brine keeps the meat moist, and the dual-breadcrumb coating delivers that deep-fried crunch without the mess. Ready in 20 minutes, loved by everyone at the table.

The air fryer has quietly changed the way home cooks approach fried food. The technology is not new — convection ovens have been circulating hot air around food for decades — but the compact, countertop format and the speed at which these machines work have turned them into one of the most-used appliances in Gulf kitchens. In a region where deep-fried snacks are a staple of everything from school lunchboxes to Ramadan iftar spreads, the air fryer offers a genuine alternative: crispy results with a fraction of the oil.

Chicken tenders sit in a sweet spot for air fryer cooking. They are thin enough to cook through in minutes, but substantial enough to develop a proper crust. The challenge has always been getting breaded chicken to crisp evenly in an air fryer without the coating falling off or turning pale and soft. The solution is a three-step dredge — flour, egg, breadcrumbs — with a firm press at each stage, and a light mist of oil before and during cooking. That oil spray is not optional. Without it, the breadcrumbs toast unevenly and the surface stays matte instead of golden.

The brine is the other key. Chicken tenders are lean, and lean meat dries out fast at high heat. A 30-minute soak in salted water seasons the meat all the way through and adds enough moisture to keep the interior juicy even at 200°C. It is a small step that makes a significant difference, and once you try it, you will not skip it again.

Air Fryer Chicken Tenders

Scale:

Ingredients

Brine

  • 4 cups water
  • 2 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar

Chicken & Coating

  • 1 lbs chicken tenders (about 680g — or chicken breast cut into strips)
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs (whisked)
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • ½ cup regular breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp salt
  • cooking oil spray (avocado or olive oil)

Instructions

  1. 1

    Whisk together the water, kosher salt, and sugar in a large bowl until fully dissolved. Submerge the chicken tenders in the brine and refrigerate for 30 minutes. Even 15 minutes helps if you are short on time.

    Brining seasons the meat throughout and adds moisture that survives the high heat of the air fryer. Do not skip this step.

  2. 2

    Preheat your air fryer to 200°C (400°F). Preheating ensures the chicken starts crisping the moment it hits the basket.

  3. 3

    Set up three shallow bowls. Bowl one: flour. Bowl two: whisked eggs. Bowl three: panko breadcrumbs, regular breadcrumbs, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, black pepper, and salt, mixed together.

  4. 4

    Remove the chicken from the brine and pat each piece thoroughly dry with paper towels. This is crucial — excess moisture prevents the breading from adhering.

    Take your time here. Damp chicken means the flour slides off and the coating falls apart during cooking.

  5. 5

    Dredge each tender in flour, shaking off the excess. Dip into the egg wash, letting any excess drip back. Press firmly into the breadcrumb mixture, coating all sides. Use your hands to really pack the crumbs on — no bare spots.

  6. 6

    Lightly spray the air fryer basket with cooking oil. Arrange the breaded tenders in a single layer with space between each piece. Do not overcrowd — work in batches if needed. Spray the tops of the tenders with oil.

  7. 7

    Air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 8 minutes. Flip the tenders, spray lightly with oil again, and cook for another 4 to 6 minutes until golden brown and the internal temperature reaches 74°C (165°F).

  8. 8

    Remove from the air fryer and let rest for 2 minutes. Serve with honey mustard, ranch, barbecue sauce, or ketchup.

Tips

  • Do not skip the brine. A 30-minute soak keeps the chicken moist at high air fryer temperatures. At minimum, season the chicken with salt and sugar and let it sit while you prepare the rest.
  • Pat the chicken completely dry after brining. This is the single most important step for getting the coating to stick.
  • Press the breadcrumbs firmly with your hands. Light dusting will not hold — you need to pack the coating on.
  • Do not overcrowd the basket. Overlapping tenders will steam instead of crisp. Cook in batches and keep finished tenders warm in a 100°C (200°F) oven.
  • Oil spray is essential. A light mist on the breaded chicken is what turns the coating golden and crispy. Without it, the breadcrumbs stay pale.
  • Use a meat thermometer. Air fryer temperatures vary between models. Cook until the internal temperature reaches 74°C (165°F) for guaranteed juicy results.

Substitutions

  • No chicken tenders? Cut boneless, skinless chicken breast into strips about 2cm wide. They work identically.
  • For gluten-free tenders, use a gluten-free flour blend and crushed cornflakes or gluten-free panko in place of regular breadcrumbs.
  • No panko? Use all regular breadcrumbs — the texture will be slightly less shatteringly crisp but still good.
  • Swap paprika for smoked paprika for a subtle smoky flavor, or add a pinch of cayenne for heat.

Storage

Store leftover tenders in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in the air fryer at 180°C (350°F) for 3 to 4 minutes to restore crispiness. Do not microwave — it will make the coating soggy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use frozen chicken tenders?

Yes, but thaw them completely first and pat dry. Cooking from frozen will result in uneven cooking and the breading will not adhere properly.

Why is my air fryer chicken not crispy?

Three common reasons: the chicken was not dried properly after brining, the basket was overcrowded (causing steaming), or you skipped the oil spray. All three are essential for a crispy result.

Can I make these ahead for meal prep?

Yes. Bread the tenders and freeze them uncooked on a baking sheet. Once frozen, transfer to a zip-lock bag. Cook from frozen, adding 2 to 3 extra minutes to the cooking time.

What temperature should I use for my air fryer?

200°C (400°F) works for most air fryers. If your model runs hot, reduce to 190°C (375°F) and add a minute to the cook time. Use a meat thermometer to confirm doneness at 74°C (165°F).
