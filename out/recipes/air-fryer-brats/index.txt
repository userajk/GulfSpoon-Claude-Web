Drain, pat them dry, then air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 4 to 6 minutes to brown the outside. Simmering is optional; the straight air-fryer method cooks them through safely on its own.</p>
<p>If you&#39;d rather skip the peppers, cook the brats over thick roasted onion wedges instead, using the same 5-minute head start.
