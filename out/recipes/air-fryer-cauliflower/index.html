Roasted Garlic Parmesan Air Fryer Cauliflower

By Sarah Al-Fayed|Updated: September 24, 2026
Prep 5 mins
Cook 15 mins
Total 20 mins
Servings 4
Difficulty Easy
Cuisine International

Cauliflower florets roasted in the air fryer until the edges are crispy and golden, tossed in olive oil, garlic, and a generous shower of parmesan. The air fryer caramelises cauliflower faster and more evenly than a conventional oven, turning an everyday vegetable into something people actually fight over at the dinner table.

Cauliflower is one of the most underrated vegetables for the air fryer. The circulating heat hits every surface of each floret, crisping the edges to a deep golden brown while keeping the interior tender and slightly nutty. In a conventional oven, this level of caramelisation takes 30 to 40 minutes and usually requires flipping. In the air fryer, you get there in 15 minutes with a single shake of the basket.

The garlic and parmesan here are not just seasoning — they are architecture. The parmesan melts into a thin, crispy crust on the surface of each floret, and the garlic toasts to a sweet, mellow flavor that bears no resemblance to the sharp bite of raw garlic. Together, they transform plain cauliflower into something that genuinely competes with fries and chips for snacking appeal.

The trick to great air fryer cauliflower is not overcrowding the basket. Cauliflower releases moisture as it cooks, and if the florets are packed in too tightly, they steam instead of roasting. The difference between crowded cauliflower (soft, pale, slightly mushy) and properly spaced cauliflower (crispy, golden, caramelised) is dramatic.

In Gulf kitchens, cauliflower has always been popular — used in everything from Indian-influenced gobi dishes to Arabic-style fried cauliflower with tahini. The air fryer version adds a Western twist with parmesan while keeping the simplicity that makes cauliflower a weeknight staple.

Pro Tip: Add the parmesan during the last 3 minutes of cooking, not at the start. This prevents it from burning and gives you a golden, crispy cheese crust.

Air Fryer Garlic Parmesan Cauliflower

Scale:

Ingredients

  • 1 medium head cauliflower (about 600g, cut into bite-sized florets)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ⅓ cup grated parmesan cheese (about 30g — add during last 3 minutes)
  • fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)
  • lemon wedges (for serving)

Instructions

  1. 1

    Cut the cauliflower into evenly sized bite-sized florets. Smaller, similar-sized pieces cook more evenly. Pat dry if you washed them.

    Dry florets roast; wet florets steam. A few seconds with a paper towel makes a real difference.

  2. 2

    In a large bowl, toss the cauliflower florets with olive oil, minced garlic, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and black pepper until evenly coated.

  3. 3

    Preheat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F).

  4. 4

    Arrange the cauliflower in a single layer in the air fryer basket. Do not overcrowd — cook in batches if needed. Leave space between florets for air circulation.

  5. 5

    Air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 10 minutes. Shake the basket to redistribute.

  6. 6

    Sprinkle the grated parmesan evenly over the cauliflower. Cook for another 3 to 5 minutes until the cauliflower is golden brown at the edges and the parmesan has melted into a crispy crust.

    Adding parmesan at the end prevents burning. At 200°C for the full cook time, it would turn bitter.

  7. 7

    Remove from the air fryer. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley and a squeeze of lemon juice. Serve immediately while the parmesan crust is still crispy.

Tips

  • Do not overcrowd the basket. This is the most common mistake. Crowded cauliflower steams and turns soggy instead of crisping.
  • Cut florets to similar sizes for even cooking. Tiny pieces will burn before large ones are done.
  • Add the parmesan in the last 3 minutes only. It burns quickly at high temperatures.
  • Pat the florets dry before tossing with oil. Surface moisture prevents caramelisation.
  • Shake the basket once during cooking for even browning on all sides.

Substitutions

  • No parmesan? Use nutritional yeast for a vegan version that still gives a savoury, cheesy flavour.
  • Swap smoked paprika for regular paprika or a pinch of cumin.
  • Use broccoli florets instead of cauliflower — same method, same cook time.
  • For a Middle Eastern twist, replace parmesan with za'atar and serve with tahini drizzle.

Storage

Store leftovers in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in the air fryer at 190°C (375°F) for 3 to 4 minutes. Microwaving works but softens the crispy edges.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does cauliflower take in the air fryer?

13 to 15 minutes at 200°C (400°F). Shake the basket at the 10-minute mark and add parmesan for the final 3 minutes.

Why is my air fryer cauliflower not crispy?

Most likely the basket was overcrowded. Cauliflower releases moisture as it cooks, and too many florets packed together causes steaming instead of roasting. Cook in batches with space between pieces.

Can I use frozen cauliflower?

Yes, but it will not be as crispy as fresh. Do not thaw it — cook from frozen, adding 3 to 5 extra minutes to the cook time. The excess moisture from freezing makes it harder to achieve crispy edges.

Is air fryer cauliflower healthy?

Very. Cauliflower is low in calories and high in fiber, vitamin C, and vitamin K. Air frying uses minimal oil compared to roasting or deep frying. A serving of this recipe has about 120 calories including the parmesan.

What other seasonings work for air fryer cauliflower?

Almost anything. Popular options include curry powder, za'atar, ranch seasoning, cajun spice, or a simple lemon-herb blend. Cauliflower is a blank canvas that takes on whatever flavour you give it.
