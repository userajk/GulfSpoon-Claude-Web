Homemade Latte: How to Make a Latte at Home

By Sarah Al-Fayed|Updated: October 24, 2024
Prep 5 mins
Cook 5 mins
Total 10 mins
Servings 1
Difficulty Easy
Cuisine International

A cafe-quality latte made at home with simple techniques and quality ingredients.

Homemade Latte

Scale:

Ingredients

For the Latte

  • 2 oz freshly pulled espresso (1 double shot)
  • 8 oz whole milk (or preferred alternative like oat milk)
  • 1 tsp simple syrup or vanilla extract (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1

    Brew your espresso into a wide, pre-warmed mug. If using sweetener, stir it in now while the coffee is hot.

  2. 2

    Steam the milk until it reaches roughly 150°F (65°C), aiming for a smooth, velvety microfoam. Tap the pitcher on the counter to break large bubbles.

  3. 3

    Pour the milk slowly into the center of the espresso from slightly higher up to mix, then lower the pitcher close to the surface to layer the foam on top.

Tips

  • Pre-warm your mug with hot water for better temperature retention.
  • If you don't have an espresso machine, strong coffee from a moka pot or AeroPress works well.
  • Whole milk froths best, but oat milk is the best dairy-free alternative for microfoam.

Substitutions

  • Replace espresso with 2 oz of very strong brewed coffee from a moka pot.
  • Use oat, almond, or soy milk instead of dairy. Barista-edition plant milks froth better.

Storage

Best served immediately. Latte cannot be stored.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make a latte without an espresso machine?

Yes — use a moka pot, AeroPress, or strong French press coffee as the base. The key is concentrated coffee.

What milk froths the best?

Whole milk produces the creamiest microfoam. For dairy-free, barista-edition oat milk is the closest alternative.
