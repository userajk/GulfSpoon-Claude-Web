Air Fryer Chicken Wings: Extra-Crispy with No Deep Frying

By Sarah Al-Fayed|Updated: September 24, 2026
Prep 10 mins
Cook 28 mins
Total 38 mins
Servings 4
Difficulty Easy
Cuisine International

Extra-crispy chicken wings with shattering golden skin and tender, juicy meat — made in the air fryer with no deep frying and barely any oil. A baking powder trick draws moisture from the skin for maximum crunch. Toss in buffalo, honey garlic, or salt-and-pepper seasoning.

Wings are one of the hardest things to get right without a deep fryer — and one of the easiest things to get right with an air fryer. The circulating heat renders the fat under the skin and crisps it from every angle, producing results that rival restaurant-quality fried wings. The secret weapon is baking powder. A light dusting mixed into the seasoning raises the skin's pH, which breaks down proteins and draws out moisture during cooking. The result is skin that crisps faster and stays crunchier longer.

The technique requires patience in one area: temperature staging. Starting the wings at a lower temperature (180°C) renders the fat slowly and evenly. Finishing at a higher temperature (220°C) blasts the skin to a deep golden crunch. This two-stage approach is what separates great air fryer wings from merely good ones.

In the Gulf, chicken wings have become a fixture at gatherings — from Friday family dinners to football watch parties. Every shawarma shop and casual restaurant has them on the menu, and the home air fryer has made it possible to produce the same quality on a weeknight. The three sauces below — classic buffalo, honey garlic, and dry salt-and-pepper — cover the range from fiery to sweet to simple.

Pro Tip: Use a wire rack if your air fryer has one. Elevating the wings allows rendered fat to drip away, keeping the bottoms crispy instead of sitting in grease.

Air Fryer Chicken Wings

Scale:

Ingredients

Wings

  • 1 kg chicken wings (about 2 lbs — split into flats and drumettes, tips removed)
  • 1 tbsp baking powder (not baking soda — aluminium-free preferred)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • cooking oil spray (light mist)

Buffalo Sauce (Option 1)

  • ⅓ cup hot sauce (Frank's RedHot or similar)
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder

Honey Garlic Sauce (Option 2)

  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 cloves garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • ½ tsp sesame oil

Salt & Pepper Seasoning (Option 3)

  • ½ tsp coarse salt
  • 1 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
  • ¼ tsp white pepper
  • ½ tsp garlic powder

Instructions

  1. 1

    Pat the chicken wings completely dry with paper towels. This is critical — any surface moisture prevents the skin from crisping.

    For maximum crispiness, dry the wings and leave them uncovered on a plate in the fridge for 1 to 4 hours before cooking. This air-dries the skin.

  2. 2

    In a large bowl, toss the wings with baking powder, salt, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper until evenly coated. Make sure every wing is covered.

    The baking powder is the crunch secret. It raises the pH of the skin, which helps it brown faster and crisp harder. Do not substitute baking soda — it will leave a metallic taste.

  3. 3

    Preheat your air fryer to 180°C (360°F).

  4. 4

    Arrange the wings in a single layer in the air fryer basket. Do not overlap or stack. Lightly spray with cooking oil. Cook in batches if needed.

  5. 5

    Air fry at 180°C (360°F) for 12 minutes. This lower temperature renders the fat slowly.

  6. 6

    Flip the wings, increase temperature to 220°C (425°F), and cook for another 12 to 16 minutes until the skin is deep golden brown and very crispy.

  7. 7

    While the wings cook, prepare your chosen sauce. For buffalo: whisk hot sauce, melted butter, and garlic powder. For honey garlic: warm honey, soy sauce, garlic, rice vinegar, and sesame oil in a small saucepan until combined. For salt and pepper: mix all dry seasonings.

  8. 8

    Remove the wings from the air fryer and immediately toss with your chosen sauce in a large bowl. Serve hot with celery sticks and your preferred dipping sauce.

Tips

  • The baking powder trick is essential. It produces crunchier skin than oil alone. Use aluminium-free baking powder if you can find it — it has a cleaner taste.
  • Do not skip the two-temperature method. Low heat first renders fat; high heat finishes with crunch.
  • Flip the wings only once, at the midpoint. Over-handling knocks off the crispy coating.
  • Do not overcrowd the basket. Wings need space for air to circulate. Cook in two batches if necessary and keep the first batch warm in a 100°C (200°F) oven.
  • For even crispier results, leave the seasoned wings uncovered in the fridge for 2 to 4 hours before cooking. The dry air removes surface moisture.
  • Quick Reference — Air Fryer Cooking Chart: Chicken Wings → 180°C for 12 min + 220°C for 12–16 min. Chicken Thighs → 200°C for 20–22 min. Chicken Tenders → 200°C for 12–14 min.

Substitutions

  • No baking powder? You can skip it, but the skin will not be as crispy. Increase the final high-heat cook time by 2 to 3 minutes.
  • Swap buffalo sauce for any hot sauce you prefer — sriracha, harissa, or even a Gulf-style shatta.
  • For dairy-free buffalo sauce, use olive oil instead of butter.
  • Use chicken drumettes only if you prefer — they cook in the same time as flats.

Storage

Store leftover wings in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat in the air fryer at 200°C (400°F) for 5 minutes to restore crispiness. Microwaving makes them soggy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you use baking powder on chicken wings?

Baking powder raises the pH of the chicken skin, which breaks down proteins and draws out moisture. This allows the skin to crisp faster and stay crunchier longer — similar to the effect of deep frying.

Do I need to flip air fryer chicken wings?

Yes, once. Flip them at the halfway point when you increase the temperature. This ensures both sides get direct exposure to the heating element for even crisping.

How long do chicken wings take in the air fryer?

About 24 to 28 minutes total using the two-temperature method: 12 minutes at 180°C (360°F) to render fat, then 12 to 16 minutes at 220°C (425°F) to crisp.

Can I cook frozen chicken wings in the air fryer?

Yes, but add 5 to 8 minutes to the total cook time. Start at 180°C for 15 minutes, flip, then finish at 220°C for 12 to 16 minutes. Season after the first stage when the surface has thawed.

Are air fryer wings healthier than deep-fried wings?

Significantly. Air fryer wings use little to no added oil, and the rendered fat drips away during cooking. A serving of air fryer wings has roughly 40 to 50 percent less fat than deep-fried wings.
