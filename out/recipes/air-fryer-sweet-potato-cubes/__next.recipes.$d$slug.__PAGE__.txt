34:T49e,The quickest route to good air fryer potatoes is a bag of baby potatoes cut in half. The skins are thin enough to leave on and the waxy flesh turns creamy rather than dry. Each half also has a flat face that browns hard against the hot basket. They take 15 to 18 minutes at 200°C (400°F) with almost no knife work.

If all you have is large potatoes, cut them into even 2.5cm cubes and follow the same method: rinse, dry, oil, season, shake. Timings for every cut are in the chart, from whole mini potatoes to smashed. For thick, fluffy-centred spears for dipping, see our [seasoned air fryer potato wedges](/recipes/air-fryer-potato-wedges/), and for a whole spud with a split top and toppings, our [air fryer baked potato with crispy salted skin](/recipes/air-fryer-baked-potato/).

In Gulf supermarkets, baby potatoes often come in net bags with sizes from marble to almost 5cm. Spend a minute sorting them before you cut: leave the smallest whole, halve the middle sizes and quarter the biggest, so every piece is about the same thickness. For a sweeter side in the same basket, try [diced sweet potato with smoked paprika or cinnamon](/recipes/air-fryer-sweet-potato-cubes/).35:T4f4,Halved baby potatoes take 15 to 18 minutes at 200°C (400°F), and 2.5cm cubes of large potatoes take 18 to 22 minutes. Whole baby potatoes take longer, 20 to 25 minutes, because the skin slows moisture loss and heat has further to travel to the centre. That is also why whole ones come out creamy and soft while halves come out crisp.

Size matters more than variety. Every extra centimetre adds roughly 5 minutes. Baby gold potatoes are slightly drier and brown a touch faster; baby red potatoes are waxier and need a minute or two more before they feel tender.

Load is the other variable. Halves need a single layer. Cubes cope with two layers if you shake hard. Fill the basket to the brim and the top layer shields the bottom one, so you get pale, steamed potatoes underneath even after 30 minutes. For six people, two batches are faster in total than one crammed basket. Oven-style air fryers with trays sit the food further from the element, so add 3 to 5 minutes and swap the trays halfway.

For dinner from one machine, cook the potatoes first and keep them warm on a tray in a low oven, then use the hot basket for [crispy-skinned air fryer chicken thighs](/recipes/air-fryer-chicken-thighs/) or [salmon fillets with crisp skin](/recipes/air-fryer-salmon/).36:T43f,Any of them will cook well. They differ in texture, and in how well they survive being shaken.

- **Baby potatoes (gold, red or purple):** young, waxy and thin-skinned. Creamy inside and crisp on the cut face, with no peeling. The least work and the most reliable batch.
- **Yukon Gold and other all-rounders:** medium starch with a buttery interior. Cubes hold their shape, and the thin skin can stay on.
- **Russet and other floury potatoes (Maris Piper, King Edward):** high starch, low moisture. Cubes roughen and crisp hard on the outside and turn fluffy inside, but the corners can break when shaken. The broken bits go extra crunchy.
- **Large red-skinned and other waxy potatoes:** hold their shape but brown less and stay dense inside when cubed.

Loose potatoes in Gulf supermarkets are often labelled by country of origin rather than variety. To check, cut one in half and rub the faces together. If they stick and leave a white, starchy film, the potato is floury and will crisp well as cubes. If the faces feel wet and slide, it is waxy and better halved or cut into chunks.37:T44e,No. Halved baby potatoes and 2.5cm cubes cook through in the air fryer alone. Boiling first is worth it in two cases. **Parboiling cubes** for 6 minutes, then steam-drying and shaking them in the colander, roughens the surface into a thicker, glassier crust like a Sunday roast potato and cuts the air fryer time to 15 to 18 minutes. **Smashed potatoes** need boiling, because the potato has to be fully tender before you flatten it.
