Air Fryer Sweet Potato Cubes, Savoury or Sweet

By Sarah Al-Fayed|Updated: November 13, 2026
Prep 10 mins
Cook 15 mins
Total 25 mins
Servings 4
Difficulty Easy
Cuisine International

Diced sweet potato roasted in the air fryer until the edges caramelise and the centres turn soft. One base method, two seasonings: smoky and savoury for dinner, or cinnamon and date syrup for breakfast bowls.

Quick answer

Cook 2cm (¾ inch) sweet potato cubes at 200°C (400°F) for 12 to 15 minutes, shaking every 5 minutes. Toss them with 1 tablespoon of oil and 2 teaspoons of cornflour per 600g first. They are done when the edges are dark and caramelised in spots and a fork slides in easily.

Sweet potato cubes are the quickest way to get roasted sweet potato on the table: diced to 2cm, they cook in 12 to 15 minutes, about half the time a whole one needs. This page is about the diced version you can scatter over salads, grain bowls and breakfast plates. For whole baked sweet potatoes or long-cut fries, see our whole baked sweet potatoes and sweet potato fries recipe.

Sweet potato has more sugar and less starch than ordinary potato, so in hot air the outside caramelises and darkens quickly while the inside goes soft and almost jammy. It will never crunch the way crispy cubed white potatoes do. A light dusting of cornflour gets you the next best thing: a thin, firm skin with dark, sticky edges.

Season them savoury with smoked paprika and cumin, or sweet with cinnamon and a drizzle of date syrup added at the very end. The method doesn't change.

How long to cook sweet potato cubes in the air fryer

Cubes of 2cm take 12 to 15 minutes at 200°C (400°F). Smaller 1.5cm dice take 10 to 12 minutes, and 2.5cm cubes take 15 to 18.

That is several minutes quicker than ordinary potato cut to the same size, because sweet potato flesh softens sooner. Its sugar also browns faster, so the gap between caramelised and scorched is short. Start checking 2 minutes before the shortest time in the chart.

Frozen diced sweet potato from the supermarket goes straight in without thawing. Cook it for 5 minutes on its own to drive off ice, then toss with oil and seasoning and cook for another 9 to 13 minutes.

Why do sweet potato cubes go soft instead of crisp?

Sweet potato has less starch than ordinary potato, and some of what it has turns to sugar as it heats. Starch is what dries into a crunchy shell, so roasted sweet potato comes out soft and caramelised instead.

Four things push it further towards crisp:

  • Cornflour. Two teaspoons per 600g, tossed on after the oil, adds the starch that sweet potato lacks and dries into a thin, firm skin.
  • Dry surfaces. Pat the cubes dry before oiling.
  • Space. A packed basket traps steam. Keep to a single layer or a loose double one.
  • A short rest. Two minutes in the open basket lets steam escape and the outsides firm up.

White-fleshed sweet potatoes (often purple- or red-skinned and sold as Japanese or Murasaki sweet potatoes) are drier and starchier than orange ones. They crisp noticeably better but taste less sweet. Give them a minute or two longer.

Should you peel sweet potatoes before cubing?

Peeling is optional. Skin-on cubes keep their shape better, add fibre and give a slightly chewy edge, and they save 5 minutes. Scrub them hard, because soil sits in the small grooves, and trim any dark or woody spots.

Peel them for the sweet version or for small children, since the cinnamon and date syrup taste cleaner without the earthy skin. Peel them too if the skin is thick, cracked or wrinkled from long storage.

Ways to use air-fried sweet potato cubes

A tub of cooked cubes keeps for 4 days and goes into most meals of the week.

  • Grain bowls: with quinoa or bulgur, crunchy roasted chickpeas, cucumber and a tahini and lemon dressing.
  • Protein plates: next to honey garlic salmon bites or crisp air fryer tofu with steamed greens.
  • Breakfast: reheat the savoury cubes and serve with fried eggs and labneh, or fold them into a pan of Gulf-style shakshuka for the last 5 minutes.
  • Iftar: the sweet version with date syrup and a spoonful of thick yoghurt makes a light way to start the meal after dates and water.
  • Salads: toss warm cubes with rocket, pomegranate seeds, crumbled feta and toasted walnuts.

Air Fryer Sweet Potato Cubes

Scale:

Ingredients

Base

  • 600 g sweet potatoes (about 2 medium (1⅓ lb), orange-fleshed)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
  • 2 tsp cornflour (cornstarch) (optional, for firmer edges)
  • ½ tsp fine salt

Savoury seasoning

  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp fresh coriander or parsley (chopped, to finish)

Or sweet seasoning

  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp ground cardamom (optional)
  • 1 tbsp date syrup or honey (added in the last 3 minutes)
  • 1 pinch flaky salt (to finish)

Instructions

  1. 1

    Peel the sweet potatoes, or scrub them well if you want to keep the skin. Cut into 1cm-thick slabs, then into 2cm (¾ inch) cubes.

    Sweet potatoes are dense and can roll. Halve each one lengthways first so it sits flat on the board.

  2. 2

    Pat the cubes dry with a tea towel. Cut sweet potato weeps sticky juice, and surface moisture steams the cubes instead of browning them.

  3. 3

    Preheat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F) for 3 minutes.

  4. 4

    Toss the cubes with the oil first, then sprinkle over the cornflour and salt and toss again until no white powder is visible. Add the savoury spices now, or the cinnamon and cardamom for the sweet version.

    Oil before cornflour. If the cornflour goes on first it clumps into pasty spots that stay chalky.

  5. 5

    Spread the cubes in the basket in a single layer, or at most a loose double layer. In a 5 to 6 litre basket, 600g fits. In a smaller basket, cook in two batches.

  6. 6

    Air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 12 to 15 minutes, shaking every 5 minutes. For the sweet version, drizzle over the date syrup or honey at the 12-minute mark, toss, and cook for the final 2 to 3 minutes.

  7. 7

    Check for doneness: the corners should be dark brown in spots and a fork should slide into a cube with no resistance. If they are tender but pale, give them 2 more minutes.

  8. 8

    Rest in the basket, drawer open, for 2 minutes. Steam escapes and the outsides firm up. Finish with fresh herbs (savoury) or flaky salt (sweet).

Equipment

Air fryer (5 to 6 litre basket) · Mixing bowl · Sharp knife and chopping board · Tea towel

Tips

  • Cut evenly. A 1cm cube and a 3cm cube cooked together means burnt crumbs and raw chunks.
  • Add honey or date syrup only at the end. Sugars burn fast at 200°C and turn bitter.
  • Pick sweet potatoes of similar thickness along their length. Tapered ends produce small pieces that burn, so cut those slightly thicker.
  • Sweet potatoes keep longer in a cool, dark cupboard than in the fridge. Cold storage can make the flesh hard in the centre after cooking.
  • If your air fryer runs hot or is a small 2 to 3 litre model, drop to 190°C (375°F) and add 2 minutes.

Troubleshooting

  • Mushy and pale: too crowded or too wet. Pat dry, add cornflour and cook in two batches.
  • Burnt edges, firm centres: cubes too large or the air fryer runs hot. Cut to 2cm and drop to 190°C (375°F).
  • Bitter, blackened glaze: honey or syrup went in too early. Add it only for the last 2 to 3 minutes.
  • Chalky white patches: cornflour went on before the oil. Toss in oil first.
  • Hard spots in the centre after cooking: the sweet potatoes were stored in the fridge. Store them at cool room temperature instead.

Substitutions

  • No cornflour? Use the same amount of potato starch or arrowroot, or leave it out for softer cubes.
  • Swap smoked paprika for Aleppo pepper or Kashmiri chilli powder.
  • Use maple syrup instead of date syrup or honey.
  • Replace cinnamon and cardamom with a mixed spice or pumpkin spice blend.

Variations

  • Za'atar and feta: season with 1 tsp za'atar instead of paprika and cumin, then finish with crumbled feta and a drizzle of olive oil.
  • Chilli and lime: use ½ tsp chilli powder with the cumin, then finish with lime zest and juice.
  • Cajun: replace the savoury seasoning with 1½ tsp Cajun spice and a pinch of brown sugar.
  • Cinnamon and tahini: cook the sweet version, then drizzle with tahini and date syrup and scatter with sesame seeds.

What to Serve With It

  • With grilled chicken thighs and a garlic yoghurt sauce.
  • In a wrap with black beans, avocado and pickled red onion.
  • As a snack with a chipotle mayonnaise or a sriracha and yoghurt dip.
  • Scattered over a spinach salad with goat's cheese and pomegranate molasses.

Storage

Refrigerate cooled cubes in an airtight container for up to 4 days. To freeze, spread them on a tray until firm, then bag for up to 3 months. They soften after freezing, so frozen cubes suit bowls, soups and hash better than serving as a side.

Reheating

Reheat in the air fryer at 180°C (350°F) for 4 to 5 minutes, shaking once. From frozen, allow 8 to 10 minutes. The microwave works for bowls and meal prep but softens the edges.

Make Ahead

Peel and dice the sweet potatoes up to 2 days ahead and keep them in an airtight container in the fridge. Unlike white potatoes, they don't need to sit in water. Dry them before oiling.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do you air fry sweet potato cubes?

Air fry 2cm sweet potato cubes at 200°C (400°F) for 12 to 15 minutes, shaking every 5 minutes. Smaller 1.5cm cubes take 10 to 12 minutes and 2.5cm cubes take 15 to 18 minutes. They are ready when a fork slides in easily and the edges are caramelised.

What temperature do you cook sweet potato cubes at in the air fryer?

Use 200°C (400°F) for cubes up to 2.5cm. It caramelises the outside while the inside softens. For chunks over 3cm, drop to 190°C (375°F) so the edges don't burn before the centre cooks. Add honey or date syrup only for the last 2 to 3 minutes.

How do you get sweet potato cubes crispy in the air fryer?

Dry the cubes, toss them in oil, then add 2 teaspoons of cornflour per 600g. Cook in a single layer at 200°C (400°F) and let them rest in the open basket for 2 minutes. They will be firm and caramelised outside but still soft in the middle.

Do you have to peel sweet potatoes for the air fryer?

No. Scrubbed skin-on cubes hold their shape well and cook in the same time. Peel them if the skin is thick or damaged, or for the sweet cinnamon version, which tastes cleaner without the earthy skin.

Can you air fry frozen sweet potato cubes?

Yes, straight from frozen. Cook them at 200°C (400°F) for 5 minutes without oil to drive off the ice, then toss with oil and seasoning and cook for another 9 to 13 minutes, shaking twice.

Should you soak sweet potato cubes before air frying?

No. Soaking helps ordinary potatoes by washing off loose surface starch, but sweet potatoes have less starch to begin with, so soaking only leaves them wet. Dry the cubes and use a little cornflour instead.

Are air fryer sweet potato cubes good for meal prep?

Yes. They keep for 4 days in the fridge and reheat in 4 to 5 minutes in the air fryer. Cook a double batch with the savoury seasoning, which works across bowls, salads, wraps and breakfast plates.
