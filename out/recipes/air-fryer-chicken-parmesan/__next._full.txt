31:T49d,Chicken parmesan asks for a crunchy crust with wet sauce and melted cheese sitting on top of it. The air fryer gets the crust right because the fan dries and browns the panko from every side at once. Keeping it crunchy under the topping comes down to timing: sauce and cheese go on only after the chicken is fully cooked, and they get 2 to 3 minutes, enough to melt mozzarella before the tomato has time to soak in.

The method is the same three-step dredge used for our [crumbed chicken tenders](/recipes/air-fryer-chicken-tenders/), with two changes. The panko is mixed with grated hard cheese and rubbed with a tablespoon of olive oil before it touches the chicken, so it browns evenly without patchy spraying. And the chicken is pounded to an even 1.5 cm, so the thin edges don't dry out while the thick end catches up.

The marinara is a 15-minute pan sauce of tinned tomatoes, garlic, oregano and olive oil. It contains no alcohol, and it's cooked thicker than most jarred sauces, which matters here.
